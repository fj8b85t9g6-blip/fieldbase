# Brand Voice

## Provenance

- Authorized first-party samples: `.agents/product-marketing.md`, `business-docs/marketing-pack.md`, the live landing/demo copy, and Glenn's five July 23 FieldBase outreach threads plus their August 1 demo follow-ups.
- Channels represented: Website, product UI, cold email, founder social.
- Last reviewed: 2026-08-01

## Stable traits

- Tone: Direct, practical, contractor-aware, respectful, and specific.
- Reading level: Plain English; short enough to scan on a phone.
- Vocabulary: Job, crew, schedule, assign, closeout, invoice, paid, field work, no account, 60 seconds.
- Sentence shape: Short declarative sentences with one concrete workflow per paragraph.
- Openings: Lead with a specific observed workflow or a real product change, not a generic compliment.
- Closings: One low-friction action or one feedback question, followed by Glenn's name and an opt-out for cold email.
- Phrases to avoid: Revolutionary, game-changing, effortless, guaranteed, AI-powered everything, best-in-class, completely automated.
- Claim boundaries: Do not claim a real contractor uses or endorses FieldBase unless documented. Do not claim marketplace sync, automatic invoicing, payment, or AI extraction without current verification.

## Channel differences

- Email: 80–130 words, personalized first sentence, one link, one question, explicit opt-out.
- Long-form content: Checklist-oriented, practical steps, clear operational boundary, demo CTA.
- Social: Founder voice; describe what was built, who it is for, and what the reader can try. No engagement bait or inflated proof.

## Held-out validation

- Samples tested: Five prior cold emails and five demo follow-ups were inspected for consistency and duplicate suppression.
- Corrections that generalized: Lead with the recipient's actual work; replace trial-first asks with the no-account demo; preserve attribution in every link.
