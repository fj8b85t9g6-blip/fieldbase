# FieldBase demo creative brief

- Audience: Owners of 1–5 person low-voltage and IT field-service crews.
- Entry point: Jobs scattered across marketplaces, calls, screenshots, email, and direct clients.
- Promise: See the complete job-to-invoice workflow without creating an account.
- Required copy: `One field job. Dispatch to invoice. 60 seconds.` and `No account required.`
- Visual sequence: Receive job -> Assign technician -> Complete work -> Send invoice.
- Brand: Deep navy `#1e3a5f`, blue `#2d5a8e`, white, light gray; clean operations dashboard aesthetic.
- Exclusions: No fake contractor, no testimonial, no fabricated metrics, no marketplace logos, no payment guarantee.
- Format: 3:4 portrait for LinkedIn.
- Metric: Attributed demo starts, not impressions.
