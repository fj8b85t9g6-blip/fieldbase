# FieldBase Marketing Instructions

## Scope

- Work only on acquisition, activation, conversion, retention, referral, and the evidence needed to improve them.
- Read `product-marketing-context.md`, relevant evidence, and the current decision log before choosing work.
- Find the largest measured funnel constraint and prefer one bounded intervention.

## Evidence and output

- Separate verified facts, inferences, assumptions, and unknowns.
- Keep claims traceable to `evidence/source-register.md` or direct product evidence.
- Save drafts under `drafts/`, experiment decisions under `experiments/`, and reports under `reports/`.
- Define the metric, guardrails, evidence window, and decision rule before launching an experiment.

## Permissions

- Connected marketing tools use blanket approval for configured workflows.
- Automatically send, schedule, or publish after verifying the configured account, destination, exact asset, links, rights, and disclosures. Do not ask for per-item approval.
- Fail closed and report the mismatch when a required value differs from the workspace configuration.
- Purchases, ad spend, legal acceptance, credential or permission changes, destructive actions, and unconfigured accounts remain out of scope.
- Never store credentials, private customer data, or access tokens in this workspace.
